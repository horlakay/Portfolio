# Store Listing Draft (en-US)

## App name

SentinelStream Analyst Desktop

## Short description

Investigate risk events, review automated fraud decisions, and operate the
SentinelStream analyst workflow from a focused Windows desktop experience.

## Full description

SentinelStream Analyst Desktop brings the SentinelStream analyst console into a
native Windows workspace designed for fraud monitoring, decision review, and
security-oriented operational workflows.

The app is built for teams that need a streamlined way to monitor transaction
risk, inspect automated decision outcomes, and support analyst-driven review
processes in modern fraud and cybersecurity environments.

### Key capabilities

- analyst-focused desktop shell for the SentinelStream console
- review decision history and investigation workflows in a dedicated workspace
- connect to a running SentinelStream backend
- support local demonstration workflows for development and evaluation
- faster launch and cleaner operator experience than a browser-only workflow

### Designed for

- fraud operations teams
- risk analysts
- cybersecurity engineering and demonstration environments
- internal product teams validating cloud-native detection workflows

### Why SentinelStream Analyst Desktop

SentinelStream Analyst Desktop is built on top of a larger event-driven fraud
detection platform. It combines a practical analyst experience with a modular
cloud-native architecture that can support rules, machine-learning scoring, and
feedback-driven decision review.

This desktop client is best suited for:

- internal analyst workbenches
- demonstration and pilot environments
- enterprise evaluation scenarios
